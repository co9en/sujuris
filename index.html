Who we are

Sujuris is a group of independent consultants with diverse business and technical expertise. We provide unique perspectives on business and digital challenges. We work with clients to create a vision and work towards building new digital products, services, and businesses to fulfill that vision. Our consultants bring deep industry and functional expertise to drive complex change, enabling organizations to grow and drive bottom-line impact.



What we do

Digital technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Big Data, Blockchain, and Cloud-native drive new digital and transformational opportunities. The raw potential of these technologies is staggering.

Understanding how a particular technology contributes to opportunities, adapting it to the business's specific needs, and integrating it with existing systems and processes within organizations can be extremely complicated.

Sujuris understands these complexities and excels in partnering with clients to identify and prioritize digital opportunities and transform them into tangible outcomes. We help clients achieve lasting gains by capturing digital opportunities and harnessing the power of data in a scalable way. With Sujuris, it's never just technology; we bring strategy, technology implementation, and deep domain expertise to enable transformation. Sujuris focuses on three main areas: Strategy, Operational Transformation, and Commercial Transformation:

Strategy and Planning

Develop a unique vision and strategy as a basis for a strategic plan to drive growth, generate value, and create a sustainable advantage through a constellation of new digital products, services, and operations.

Operational Transformation

Identify areas that will benefit from digital technologies and work with stakeholders to transform established processes and operations through the adoption and implementation of digital technology.

Commercial Transformation

Lead cross-functional teams and engage with legal, finance, marketing, sales, product, technology, and other teams to bring unique digital value propositions to market, drive new revenue and gain a competitive advantage.
